## Description

*Welcome back to Learn With Mithran! Yesterday we sent Key Vault's audit logs into a workspace, mentioned an alert on denied access, and left an expiring secret unresolved — and said "that's Day 19" each time. Today we deliver: Azure Monitor, Log Analytics, KQL and real alerts, start to finish.*

By the end of this video you'll have built a real VM and web app, watched their metrics move live, pulled the one number platform metrics refuse to give you — disk space — with a real data collection rule, written KQL from your first line to a working error-rate query, and built three alert types that actually fire and email you. Part One collects and queries: metrics, the activity log, diagnostic settings, table plans and the true cost of Log Analytics. Part Two detects and responds: agents, alerts, action groups, Application Insights, dashboards and workbooks.

💰 This is the first day where a careless lab can genuinely bill you — the free Log Analytics grant is 5 GB per billing account per **month**, not per day, a mix-up behind one of Azure's most common surprise invoices. Today's lab stays in the pennies, and every step that isn't free is flagged. 📊
